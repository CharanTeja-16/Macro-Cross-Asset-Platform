ANALYTICAL FRAMEWORK

Macroeconomic Principles & Risk Management Mechanics

Principle 01: 2-Year Sovereign Yield Duration vs. 10-Year Transmission
The 2-year sovereign yield directly prices central bank policy decisions over the forward 24-month horizon. Because foreign exchange rates function as interest rate arbitrage mechanisms, 2-year yield spreads demonstrate superior empirical correlation with spot currency moves compared to 10-year yields, which are heavily distorted by fiscal debt supply and term premiums.
Principle 02: Negative Skewness & Asymmetric Carry Unwind Mechanics
Carry trade portfolios display positive expected yield during low-volatility regimes, but exhibit pronounced negative skewness. Violent carry liquidations occur when: (1) 2-year sovereign yield spreads compress sharply due to monetary policy shifts, or (2) surges in global FX implied volatility force leveraged funds into concurrent margin-call rushes to buy back funding currencies.
Principle 03: Risk-Parity Volatility Normalization (14-Day ATR Framework)
Fixed contract lot sizing imposes uneven capital risk across currency pairs of differing volatility regimes. Sizing by the reciprocal of the 14-day Average True Range (ATR) normalizes dollar loss to a uniform 1.0% risk budget per setup, decoupling portfolio survivability from market volatility shocks and preserving long-term risk asymmetry.
